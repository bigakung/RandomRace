# The ground a Vehicle rides on is provided by the Theme, not fixed by the engine

Adding a second Theme (elephant, on dry land) exposed that the "engine only knows Lanes and Vehicles" claim (CONTEXT.md, ADR-0002's spirit) wasn't quite true: the river — its wave shader, and the wave-based bob/pitch every Vehicle rode — was hardcoded into the shared renderer (`RaceScene3D` rendered `<River>` directly; `VehicleFleet` called a water-specific `waveHeight` function directly), not supplied per Theme.

`SceneTheme` now has two more members alongside `Vehicle` and `Environment`: a `Surface` component (the ground itself — translucent water for Ayutthaya, a flat dirt track for elephant) and a `surfaceHeightAt(x, z, seconds)` function every Vehicle rides on (a wave field for water, a constant `0` for flat ground). `VehicleFleet`'s per-frame bob/pitch math is unchanged — it just calls whichever function the active Theme provides. The engine's own generic naming was tightened to match: `riverHalfWidth` → `courseHalfWidth`, `waterSegments`/`animatedWater` → `surfaceSegments`/`animatedSurface`, and `LightingPreset`'s `water`/`riverbed`/`bank` fields → `surfacePrimary`/`surfaceShadow`/`surroundings` (every Theme's lighting preset supplies a colour for its own ground, whatever that ground is).

This is why `src/three/scene/River.tsx` no longer exists: it moved to `src/themes/ayutthaya/scenery/AyutthayaSurface.tsx`, becoming the Ayutthaya Theme's own `Surface` rather than something every Theme was stuck with. A reader who later finds a Theme's Surface component living under `src/themes/<theme>/scenery/` instead of `src/three/scene/` should read this as deliberate: anything genuinely engine-generic stays in `src/three/`; anything that assumes a particular kind of ground belongs to the Theme.
