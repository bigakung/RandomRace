# Ayutthaya Random Picker

A themed name picker: the user enters a list of names, one is chosen at random, and the choice is revealed as a boat race on a river in old Ayutthaya.

## Language

**Participant**:
One entry in the **Roster**, identified by its position number (#1, #2, …) rather than its name. Two Participants may share a name; the number tells them apart.
_Avoid_: Player, entry, contestant

**Roster**:
The ordered list of **Participants** a Race is drawn from; between 2 and 50 Participants.
_Avoid_: Name list, players list

**Race**:
One round of picking: a single random draw of a **Winner** from the current **Roster**, followed by its animated reveal. A Race produces exactly one Winner and no other ranking.
_Avoid_: Game, round, draw (as the noun for the whole thing)

**Lane**:
The strip of river belonging to one **Participant** during a Race.
_Avoid_: Track, row

**Vehicle**:
The theme-specific thing that carries a Participant along its Lane — a boat in the Ayutthaya theme, an elephant in a future elephant theme.
_Avoid_: Boat (outside Ayutthaya-specific art), racer, avatar

**Theme**:
A swappable look-and-feel for the Race — scenery, Vehicle art, colours and wording — that never changes how the Winner is chosen or how the Race moves.
_Avoid_: Skin, style

**Race Duration**:
How long the Race runs from GO until the **Winner** crosses the finish line; chosen by the user from fixed presets between 5 seconds and 5 minutes.
_Avoid_: Race length, timer

**Leader**:
Whichever Participant is currently furthest along during a Race. Leaders change as the Race unfolds and are for display only; they are never a result.
_Avoid_: Ranking, placing, finish order

**Winner**:
The single **Participant** chosen by the random draw before the animation starts; the animation only visualises this choice.
_Avoid_: Result, champion, first place
